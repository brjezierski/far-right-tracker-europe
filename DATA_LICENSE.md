# Data licence

The files under `data/` are derived from Wikipedia: opinion-polling tables and party infoboxes from
[English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/), which is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The derived data in this repository is therefore also licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. If you reuse it, credit
"Wikipedia contributors" and this repository, and share your derivative under the same licence.

The page URLs each country's data comes from are recorded in that country's `metadata.json`, and each party's
article in `wikipedia_url`. The history of each Wikipedia page lists its authors.

The code is separate and is licensed under the MIT licence ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
